Which whole number is divisible by both 7 and 8 without leaving a remainder?
A  168B  215C  143D  196
Answer: A. 168 ÷ 7 = 24 and 168 ÷ 8 = 21, both whole numbers. (215 ÷ 7 isn't whole; 143 ÷ 8 isn't whole; 196 ÷ 7 = 28 works but 196 ÷ 8 = 24.5 doesn't — check BOTH conditions on every choice.)
