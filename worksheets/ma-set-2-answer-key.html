A theater sold 612 tickets and wants to divide them evenly among 19 ushers to hand them out. Which expression gives the best estimate of tickets per usher?
A  612 ÷ 20B  700 ÷ 20C  600 ÷ 19D  600 ÷ 20
Answer: D. Round both numbers to the nearest hundred/ten: 612 → 600, 19 → 20. 600 ÷ 20 = 30. (700 rounds 612 the wrong direction -- 612 is much closer to 600 than 700; 612 and 19 left un-rounded are the other two traps.)

The first term of a sequence is 9. Each term after that is 7 more than the one before. What is the 8th term?
A  65B  51C  58D  72
Answer: C. nth term = first term + (n−1) × step. 8th term = 9 + (8−1) × 7 = 9 + 49 = 58. (65 comes from 8×7+9, using n instead of n−1 — count how many STEPS from the 1st term, not the term number itself.)

14.
Which whole number is divisible by both 6 and 9 without leaving a remainder?
A  132B  108C  117D  96
Answer: B. A number divisible by 6 must be even, and a number divisible by 9 has digits that add up to a multiple of 9. 108: even, and 1+0+8=9 → divisible by both (108÷6=18, 108÷9=12). 132 is even but 1+3+2=6, not divisible by 9. 117 is odd, so it fails the divisible-by-6 check right away. 96 is even but 9+6=15, not divisible by 9. Check EACH condition separately before picking.
