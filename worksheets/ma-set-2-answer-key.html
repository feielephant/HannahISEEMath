A recycling club wants to collect 600 cans this month. The bar graph shows how many cans they collected each week so far. If there are 20 members in the club, about how many MORE cans does each member need to collect, on average, to reach the goal?
A  12B  21C  30D  18
Answer: D. Collected so far: 50+70+60+60 = 240. Still needed: 600 − 240 = 360. Split among 20 members: 360 ÷ 20 = 18. (Trap: 600 ÷ 20 = 30 skips subtracting what's already collected.)
