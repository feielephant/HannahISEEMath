Use the number line. The hash marks are evenly spaced, but only two of them are labeled. What number does M stand for?
A  20B  16C  24D  18
Answer: A. From 12 to 28 is 4 ticks, so each tick is (28 − 12) ÷ 4 = 4. M is 2 ticks after 12: 12 + 2×4 = 20. Trap: 16 and 24 come from counting one tick off.
