The floor of Room A is in the shape of a square and has an area of 121 feet². The floor of Room B is in the shape of a rectangle and has a length of 7 feet and width of 15 feet. Which statement correctly compares the floors in the two rooms?
A  The area of the floor in Room A is the same as the area of the floor in Room B.B  The perimeter of the floor in Room A is equal to the perimeter of the floor in Room B.C  The area of the floor in Room A is less than the area of the floor in Room B.D  The perimeter of the floor in Room A is less than the perimeter of the floor in Room B.
Answer: B. Room A is a square with area 121 ft², so its side length is 11 ft and its perimeter is 4 × 11 = 44 ft. Room B's area is 7 × 15 = 105 ft² (not equal to 121, so the two area-comparison choices are both false). Room B's perimeter is 2(7+15) = 44 ft -- the SAME as Room A's perimeter, even though the areas are different. You have to compute both area AND perimeter for both rooms before you can tell which comparison is true.

14.
Two numbers are greater than 1. One is a prime number and the other is an odd number. What will always describe the product of the two numbers?
A  even numberB  prime numberC  composite numberD  odd number
Answer: C. A prime number has exactly two factors (1 and itself). Multiplying it by another number greater than 1 always creates a number with MORE than two factors -- which makes it composite, no matter which prime or odd number you pick. Example: prime 2 × odd 5 = 10 (composite). Example: prime 5 × odd 7 = 35 (odd AND composite) -- the only thing true in both examples is "composite."
