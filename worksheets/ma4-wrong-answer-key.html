1.
Which expression is equal to 36 − 8 × (6 − 3)?
A  36 − 8 × 6 − 8 × 3B  36 − (8 × 6 − 3)C  (36 − 8) × (6 − 3)D  36 − 8 × 6 + 8 × 3
Answer: D. The 8 multiplies BOTH numbers in the parentheses: 8 × (6 − 3) = 8 × 6 − 8 × 3. That whole amount is taken away from 36, and a minus sign in front of it flips the − 8 × 3 to + 8 × 3: 36 − (8 × 6 − 8 × 3) = 36 − 8 × 6 + 8 × 3. Check: 36 − 8 × 3 = 12 and 36 − 48 + 24 = 12. A forgets to flip the sign. B multiplies only the 6 by 8. C subtracts 36 − 8 first (= 84).
Ask her: What does the 8 multiply? Can you write 8 × (6 − 3) without parentheses?
MA Set 4 #8 · Her answer on 11/6: B (correct D) · Likely: 8 multiplied only the 6, not both numbers in the parentheses
2.
The price of a game is increased by 50%. The new price is then decreased by 20%. What is the overall percent increase or percent decrease from the original price?
A  percent decrease by 10%B  percent increase by 30%C  percent increase by 70%D  percent increase by 20%
Answer: D. Start with $100. Up 50%: 100 + 50 = 150. Down 20% of 150: 150 − 30 = 120. 120 is 20 more than 100, so it is a 20% increase. B just subtracts the percents (50 − 20). C adds them.
Ask her: Start with $100. What is the price after each change?
MA Set 4 #13 · Her answer on 11/6: B (correct D) · Likely: subtracted the percents: 50 − 20 = 30
3.
Ava had □ stickers and then she gave ○ of them to Ben. Now, Ben has △ more stickers than Ava had. Which expression represents the number of stickers Ben had to begin with?
A  □ + △ − ○B  □ + △ + ○C  △ − □ − ○D  □ − ○ − △
Answer: A. Ava HAD □. Ben now has △ more than that: □ + △. Before Ava gave him ○, he had ○ fewer: □ + △ − ○. Try numbers: □ = 10, ○ = 3, △ = 5. Ben now has 15, so he started with 12. Only A gives 12.
Ask her: Try □ = 10, ○ = 3, △ = 5. How many does Ben have now? Before Ava gave him some?
MA Set 4 #15 · Her answer on 11/6: D (correct A) · Likely: turned "△ more than" into minus △
4.
The base of a triangle is 10 meters (m). Which statement correctly compares the value of A to the height of the triangle? (A = 12 × base × height)
A  The value of A is always 12 the value of the height.B  The value of A is always 10 times the value of the height.C  The value of A is always 5 times the value of the height.D  The value of A can be the same as the value of the height.
Answer: C. A = 1/2 × 10 × height = 5 × height, so A is always 5 times the height. B forgets the 1/2. D would need 5 × height = height, which only happens if the height is 0.
Ask her: Put base = 10 into the formula. What is A when the height is 2? When it is 4?
MA Set 4 #17 · Her answer on 11/6: A (correct C) · Likely: took the 1/2 from the formula and left out the base
5.
In a sequence, each number is 7 more than the number before it. The 9th number in the sequence is 65. What is the 25th number in the sequence?
A  112B  170C  175D  177
Answer: D. From the 9th to the 25th number is 25 − 9 = 16 steps. 16 × 7 = 112, and 65 + 112 = 177. A is 16 × 7, one step early: it still needs adding to 65. B uses 15 steps. C is 25 × 7.
Ask her: What does 112 stand for? What does the question ask for?
MA Set 4 #18 · Her answer on 11/6: A (correct D) · Likely: stopped one step early: 112 is the jump from the 9th to the 25th, not the 25th number
6.
Sam had 12 marbles and 15 shells. He lost 14 of his marbles and 23 of his shells. How many marbles and shells does he have left?
A  9B  13C  14D  19
Answer: C. Marbles: 1/4 of 12 is 3, so 9 are left. Shells: 2/3 of 15 is 10, so 5 are left. 9 + 5 = 14. B is how many he LOST. A counts only the marbles. D takes 1/3 of the shells instead of 2/3.
Ask her: Point to the number of shells he still has.
MA Set 4 #24 · Her answer on 11/6: D (correct C) · Likely: added the shells LOST (10) instead of the shells left (5)
7.
Use the number pattern to answer the question.
4,  5,  7,  11,  19,  ...
What is the 7th number in the pattern?
A  35B  51C  67D  70
Answer: C. Write the differences: 1, 2, 4, 8. Each difference doubles, so the next ones are 16 and 32. 6th: 19 + 16 = 35. 7th: 35 + 32 = 67. Number each term as you write it, so you stop at the 7th. A is the 6th number (one step early). B adds 16 again. D doubles 35.
Ask her: Write the differences under the numbers. What is the next difference?
MA Set 4 #25 · Her answer on 11/6: D (correct C) · Likely: found the 6th number (35), then doubled 35 instead of doubling the difference
8.
A survey was done to determine which sport was preferred between soccer, football, and basketball. There were 3 times as many votes for soccer as football and 5 times as many votes for football as for basketball. If there is a total of 42 people in the survey, how many people voted for soccer?
A  24B  27C  30D  35
Answer: C. Let basketball be 1 part. Football is 5 parts and soccer is 3 × 5 = 15 parts: 1 + 5 + 15 = 21 parts = 42, so 1 part = 2. Soccer = 15 × 2 = 30.
Ask her: How did you get 27? Draw one box for basketball, then football, then soccer.
MA Set 4 #27 · Her answer on 11/6: B (correct C) · Likely: no clear source for 27 (maybe 42 − 15)
9.
Wendy can type 60 words in a minute. Wendy needs to type a 1,000-word essay and a 2,000-word short story. Which equation can be used to find t, the total time, in minutes, it will take Wendy to type both the essay and the short story?
A  t = (2,000 × 1,000) × 60B  t = (2,000 × 1,000) ÷ 60C  t = (1,000 + 2,000) × 60D  t = (1,000 + 2,000) ÷ 60
Answer: D. First the total words: 1,000 + 2,000 = 3,000. She types 60 a minute, so the minutes are 3,000 ÷ 60 = 50. The parentheses make the adding happen first. C multiplies, which would make the time bigger than the words.
Ask her: Why did you change from D? What does the total number of words mean?
MA Set 4 #28 · Her answer on 11/6: B (first picked D, then changed) (correct D) · Likely: had it right, then changed to multiplying 2,000 × 1,000
