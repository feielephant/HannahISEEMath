1.  MA6 #3
A bike costs $64. The price was increased by 25%. Then the new price was decreased by 30%. What is the price now?
Her answer on 11/20: C
Answer: A. 25% of 64 is 16, so the new price is $80. Then 30% of the NEW price: 30% of 80 is 24. 80 − 24 = $56.00. C subtracts 0.30 instead of 30% of the price (the "decreased by" slip). B takes 5% off $64 (the 25 − 30 shortcut), which is also what you get by taking 30% of the old $64 instead of the new $80. D adds the 24 instead of subtracting.
Ask her: The price went up to $80. What is 30% of $80? So what does "decreased by 30%" take away?

13.  MA6 #29 · redone after the timer
The width of a rectangle is one fourth of its length. The perimeter of the rectangle is 50 meters. What is the length of the rectangle?
Her answer on 11/20: B (redone after the timer; her timed pick was random; no time recorded)
Answer: C. Width : length = 1 : 4, so one width and one length make 5 equal parts. One width + one length = half the perimeter = 25 meters. Each part is 25 ÷ 5 = 5, so the width is 5 and the length is 4 × 5 = 20 meters. Check: 2 × (20 + 5) = 50. A is the width. B divides the whole perimeter by 5. D is the area (20 × 5), not the length.
Ask her: How did you get 10? The perimeter goes around all 4 sides. What are length + width together?
