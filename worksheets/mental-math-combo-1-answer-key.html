Mental Math: Combo 1 · Answer Key

9 problems. Combines the unused percent/brackets drill with the fraction/decimal-compare drill, trimmed to fit 10 minutes together.

Part 1 — Percent of a Number
1.
What is 20% of 65?
Answer: 13.
2.
What is 60% of 85?
Answer: 51.
Part 2 — Order of Operations with Brackets
3.
8 + 12 ÷ 4 × 3 − 5
Answer: 12. 12÷4=3, 3×3=9, 8+9=17, 17−5=12.
4.
15 − 20 ÷ (2 + 3)
Answer: 11. 2+3=5, 20÷5=4, 15−4=11.
Part 3 — Fraction & Decimal Compare
5.
35 vs. 0.55
Answer: 3/5. 3/5 = 0.6, bigger than 0.55.
6.
78 vs. 0.8
Answer: 7/8. 7/8 = 0.875, bigger than 0.8.
7.
34 vs. 57
Answer: 3/4. 3×7=21, 5×4=20. 21>20, so 3/4 is bigger.
8.
49 vs. 12
Answer: 1/2. 4×2=8, 1×9=9. 8<9, so 1/2 is bigger.
9.
Order least to greatest: 712, 0.55, 23
Answer: 0.55, 7/12, 2/3. 7/12 ≈ 0.583, 2/3 ≈ 0.667.
