9 problems. Combines the unused percent/brackets drill (set 2) with the fraction/decimal-compare drill (set 2), trimmed to fit 10 minutes together.

Part 1 — Percent of a Number
1.

Answer: 28.
2.

Part 2 — Order of Operations with Brackets
3.
30 ÷ 5 + 4 × 6 − 7
Answer: 23. 30÷5=6, 4×6=24, 6+24=30, 30−7=23.
4.
(18 − 6) ÷ 4 + 10
Answer: 13. 18−6=12, 12÷4=3, 3+10=13.
Part 3 — Fraction & Decimal Compare
5.

6.

7.

8.
