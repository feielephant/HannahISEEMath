Mental Math: Combo 5 · Answer Key

11 problems, 6 parts. Same gaps as Combo 4 with fresh numbers; sequences dropped (2/2 fast on Combo 4, 10/29). Part 4 replaces them with "increased/decreased by X%" wording (QR8 #14, high priority 10/27). Every multi-step item has a stop-early trap (intermediate value) to check the habit from Combo 4 #3 / QR8 #15.

Part 1 — Equation Substitution
1.
A = (1/2)bh. A = 36 cm², b = 8 cm. Find h.
Step 1: 36 = (1/2)(8)h, i.e. 36 = 4h. Final answer: 9. 36 ÷ 4 = 9.
2.
P = 2(l + w). P = 30 ft, l = 9 ft. Find w.
Step 1: 30 = 2(9 + w). Final answer: 6. 30 ÷ 2 = 15, 15 − 9 = 6. (Trap: forgetting the ÷ 2 gives 21 — the same step she skipped on QR8 #9.)
Part 2 — Multi-step Unit Conversion
3.
2 tobs = 12 mibs. 9 mibs = 3 rans. How many rans equal 1 tob?
Step 1: 1 tob = 6 mibs. Final answer: 2. 9 mibs = 3 rans, so 3 mibs = 1 ran. 6 mibs = 2 rans. (Trap: stopping at 6.)
4.
6 kips = 18 lons. 1 lon = 4 fets. How many fets equal 2 kips?
Step 1: 1 kip = 3 lons. Final answer: 24. 2 kips = 6 lons, 6 × 4 = 24 fets. (Traps: 12 = forgot the 2 kips; 3 or 6 = stopped at lons.)
Part 3 — Ordering More Than 2 Values
5.
Order least to greatest: 25, 0.35, 13
Answer: 1/3, 0.35, 2/5. 1/3 ≈ 0.333, 2/5 = 0.4. Same trap as Combo 4 #5: 2/5 has a small top number but is the largest.
6.
Order least to greatest: 0.65, 23, 58
Answer: 5/8, 0.65, 2/3. 5/8 = 0.625, 2/3 ≈ 0.667. All three are close, so she has to convert, not eyeball.
Part 4 — Percent Increase and Decrease
7.
A $60 jacket’s price is decreased by 30%. New price?
Answer: $42. 30% of 60 = 18; 60 − 18 = 42. (Or 70% of 60 = 42.) Trap: 18 = stopped at the change; 30 = subtracted 30 instead of 30%.
8.
A 25-member club grows by 20%. How many members now?
Answer: 30. 20% of 25 = 5; 25 + 5 = 30. Trap: 5 = stopped at the change; 45 = added 20.
Part 5 — Percent of a Number
9.
What is 80% of 55?
Answer: 44. 0.8 × 55 = 44, or 55 − 20% (11) = 44.
Part 6 — Fraction Addition: Smallest Common Denominator
10.
16 + 34
Answer: 11/12. LCD 12 (not 24): 2/12 + 9/12 = 11/12. Using 24 gives 22/24, which must be simplified.
11.
310 + 16
Answer: 7/15. LCD 30 (not 60): 9/30 + 5/30 = 14/30 = 7/15. Needs the LCD AND the final simplify.
