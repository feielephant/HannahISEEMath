Part 1 — Percent Increase and Decrease

"Decreased/increased BY X%" means X% OF the original value, not just subtracting the digits of X%. Read carefully whether the question wants the NEW amount or the amount of CHANGE.

1.
A backpack costs $40. Its price is decreased by 35%. What is the new price, in dollars?
2.
A greenhouse has 60 plants. The number of plants increases by 45%. How many plants does it have now?
3.
A town has 1,500 residents. The population decreases by 15%. How many residents does the DECREASE represent (not the new population)?
