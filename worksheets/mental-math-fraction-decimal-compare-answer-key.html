Mental Math: Fraction & Decimal Compare · Answer Key

11 problems. Built to directly target the still-open gap from MA2 #2 / QR6 #32 -- she doesn't yet have a reliable method for comparing a fraction to a decimal, or two fractions to each other.

Part 1 — Fraction vs. Decimal
1.
35 vs. 0.55
Answer: 3/5. 3/5 = 0.6, which is bigger than 0.55.
2.
25 vs. 0.45
Answer: 0.45. 2/5 = 0.4, which is smaller than 0.45.
3.

Answer: 7/8. 7/8 = 0.875, which is bigger than 0.8.
4.
13 vs. 0.4
Answer: 0.4. 1/3 ≈ 0.333, which is smaller than 0.4.
Part 2 — Fraction vs. Fraction (cross-multiply)
5.
34 vs. 57

6.

Answer: 2/3. 2×8=16, 5×3=15. 16>15, so 2/3 is bigger.
7.

Part 3 — Order three values, least to greatest
9.
