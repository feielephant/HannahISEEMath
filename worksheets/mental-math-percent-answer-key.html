Mental Math: Percent, Decimal, Fraction — Answer Key

Part 1 checks whether she has a direct shortcut for percentages she hasn't drilled (20%, 30%, 40%, 15%, 60%) the same way she does for 10%/25%/75% — not whether she can eventually get there by brute force. Part 2 checks the decimal-point-shift direction when converting between percent, decimal, and fraction.

Part 1 — Percent of a Number: find the shortcut before you multiply
1.
What is 20% of 85?
Answer: 17. 20% = 1/5, so 85÷5 = 17. (The long way: 85×20=1700, ÷100=17 — same answer, more steps and more places to slip.)
2.
What is 10% of 230?
Answer: 23. 10% = shift the decimal one place: 23.
3.
What is 30% of 140?
Answer: 42. 30% = 3×10%. 10% of 140 = 14, so 3×14 = 42.
4.
What is 25% of 96?
Answer: 24. 25% = 1/4, so 96÷4 = 24.
5.
What is 40% of 65?
Answer: 26. 40% = 4×10%. 10% of 65 = 6.5, so 4×6.5 = 26.
6.
What is 15% of 180?
Answer: 27. 15% = 10% + 5%. 10% of 180 = 18, 5% of 180 (half of 10%) = 9, so 18+9 = 27.
7.
What is 60% of 95?
Answer: 57. 60% = 6×10%. 10% of 95 = 9.5, so 6×9.5 = 57.
8.
What is 75% of 48?
Answer: 36. 75% = 3/4, so 48÷4=12, then 3×12 = 36.
Part 2 — Percent, Decimal, Fraction: converting between forms
9.
What is 0.45 as a percent?
Answer: 45%. Decimal to percent: shift the point 2 places right.
10.
What is 136% as a decimal?
Answer: 1.36. Percent to decimal: shift the point 2 places left. Over 100% means the decimal is greater than 1 — a quick sanity check.
11.
What is 3/4 as a percent?
Answer: 75%. 3/4 = 0.75, then shift the point: 75%.
12.
What is 9% as a decimal?
Answer: 0.09. Percent to decimal: shift the point 2 places left. A single-digit percent needs a leading zero after the point — 0.09, not 0.9.
13.
What is 2⅗ as a percent?
Answer: 260%. 2⅗ = 2.6, then shift the point: 260%. Being over 2 whole means the percent must be over 200%.
14.
What is 0.07 as a percent?
Answer: 7%. Decimal to percent: shift the point 2 places right: 0.07 → 7%, not 0.7%.
15.
What is 215% as a decimal?
Answer: 2.15. Percent to decimal: shift the point 2 places left. Over 100% means the decimal is greater than 1.
16.
What is 1/8 as a percent?
Answer: 12.5%. 1/8 = 0.125, then shift the point: 12.5%.
