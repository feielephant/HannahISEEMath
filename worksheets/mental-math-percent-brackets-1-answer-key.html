Mental Math: Percent & Brackets 1 · Answer Key

7 problems, 11 blanks. The bracket moves around (start, middle, end, or absent) and the surrounding operations vary too, so she can't just pattern-match "bracket, then multiply, then add" — she has to actually judge the order each time. On the worksheet, Part 2's Step labels are deliberately generic — she chooses what to calculate first and second herself. The steps below show the correct order so you can compare it to whatever order she actually used and see exactly where it diverges, not just whether the final number is right.

Part 1 — Percent of a Number
1.
What is 20% of 65?
Answer: 13. 65 × 0.2 = 13.
2.
What is 30% of 90?
Answer: 27. 90 × 0.3 = 27.
3.
What is 40% of 55?
Answer: 22. 55 × 0.4 = 22.
4.
What is 60% of 85?
Answer: 51. 85 × 0.6 = 51.
Part 2 — Order of Operations with Brackets
5.
(12 − 5) × 3 + 9
Bracket is first here — it's at the very start: 12 − 5 = 7
Then the multiplication: 7 × 3 = 21
Final (addition last): 21 + 9 = 30
6.
8 + 12 ÷ 4 × 3 − 5
No brackets here. Multiply/divide both outrank add/subtract, done left to right: 12 ÷ 4 = 3, 3 × 3 = 9
Final (add/subtract left to right): 8 + 9 = 17, 17 − 5 = 12
7.
15 − 20 ÷ (2 + 3)
Bracket is at the end this time: 2 + 3 = 5
Final: 20 ÷ 5 = 4, then 15 − 4 = 11
