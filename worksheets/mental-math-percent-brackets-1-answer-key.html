Mental Math: Percent & Brackets 1 · Answer Key

7 problems, 13 blanks. On the worksheet, Part 2's Step 1 / Step 2 labels are deliberately generic — she chooses what to calculate first and second herself. The steps below show the correct order (brackets, then multiply/divide, then the final answer) so you can compare it to whatever order she actually used and see exactly where it diverges, not just whether the final number is right.

Part 1 — Percent of a Number
1.
What is 20% of 65?
Answer: 13. 65 × 0.2 = 13.
2.
What is 30% of 90?
Answer: 27. 90 × 0.3 = 27.
3.
What is 40% of 55?
Answer: 22. 55 × 0.4 = 22.
4.
What is 60% of 85?
Answer: 51. 85 × 0.6 = 51.
Part 2 — Order of Operations with Brackets
5.
7 + 4 × (10 − 4) ÷ 3
Step 1 (brackets): 10 − 4 = 6
Step 2 (multiply & divide, left to right): 4 × 6 = 24, 24 ÷ 3 = 8
Step 3 (final): 7 + 8 = 15
6.
18 − 2 × (9 − 5) ÷ 4
Step 1 (brackets): 9 − 5 = 4
Step 2 (multiply & divide, left to right): 2 × 4 = 8, 8 ÷ 4 = 2
Step 3 (final): 18 − 2 = 16
7.
9 + 6 × (11 − 8) ÷ 2
Step 1 (brackets): 11 − 8 = 3
Step 2 (multiply & divide, left to right): 6 × 3 = 18, 18 ÷ 2 = 9
Step 3 (final): 9 + 9 = 18
