7 problems, 13 blanks. Same structure as Set 1, new numbers — a second rep on both skills. Part 2's Step 1 / Step 2 labels on the worksheet are generic; she chooses her own order. The steps below are the correct order, for comparing against whatever she actually did.

Part 1 — Percent of a Number
1.
What is 15% of 140?
Answer: 21. 140 × 0.15 = 21.
2.
What is 35% of 80?
Answer: 28. 80 × 0.35 = 28.
3.
What is 45% of 120?
Answer: 54. 120 × 0.45 = 54.
4.

Part 2 — Order of Operations with Brackets
5.
10 + 3 × (14 − 9) ÷ 5
Step 1 (brackets): 14 − 9 = 5
Step 2 (multiply & divide, left to right): 3 × 5 = 15, 15 ÷ 5 = 3
Step 3 (final): 10 + 3 = 13
6.
20 − 4 × (7 − 2) ÷ 5
Step 1 (brackets): 7 − 2 = 5
Step 2 (multiply & divide, left to right): 4 × 5 = 20, 20 ÷ 5 = 4
Step 3 (final): 20 − 4 = 16
7.
6 + 8 × (13 − 7) ÷ 4
Step 1 (brackets): 13 − 7 = 6
Step 2 (multiply & divide, left to right): 8 × 6 = 48, 48 ÷ 4 = 12
Step 3 (final): 6 + 12 = 18
