Part 1 — Percent of a Number
1.
What is 15% of 140?
Answer: 21. 140 × 0.15 = 21.
2.
What is 35% of 80?
Answer: 28. 80 × 0.35 = 28.
3.
What is 45% of 120?
Answer: 54. 120 × 0.45 = 54.
4.
What is 70% of 90?
Answer: 63. 90 × 0.7 = 63.
Part 2 — Order of Operations with Brackets
5.
20 − 3 × (7 − 4)
Bracket is in the middle, only one operation (multiply, no divide) follows it: 7 − 4 = 3
Then multiply: 3 × 3 = 9
Final (subtract last): 20 − 9 = 11
6.
30 ÷ 5 + 4 × 6 − 7
No brackets, and this time there are two SEPARATE multiply/divide terms, not next to each other: 30 ÷ 5 = 6
The other one: 4 × 6 = 24
Final (add/subtract left to right): 6 + 24 = 30, 30 − 7 = 23
7.
(18 − 6) ÷ 4 + 10
Bracket at the very start again, but this time followed by divide (not multiply): 18 − 6 = 12
Then divide: 12 ÷ 4 = 3
Final: 3 + 10 = 13
