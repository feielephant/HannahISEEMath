Mental Math: Percent Speed · Answer Key

10 problems. Percent-of-a-number only, isolated from any other skill — the point is reps on the shortcut method.

1.
What is 20% of 45?
Answer: 9. ×0.2, or ÷5.
2.
What is 30% of 70?
Answer: 21. 10% of 70 = 7, ×3 = 21.
3.
What is 40% of 55?
Answer: 22. 10% of 55 = 5.5, ×4 = 22.
4.
What is 15% of 80?
Answer: 12. 10% of 80 = 8, half of that (5%) = 4, 8+4 = 12.
5.
What is 35% of 140?
Answer: 49. 10% of 140 = 14, ×3 = 42, plus 5% (7) = 49.
