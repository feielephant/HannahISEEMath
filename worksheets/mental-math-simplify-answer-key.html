Mental Math: Simplify First — Answer Key

The point isn't just the right number — it's whether she spotted the shortcut. If she got the right answer by brute-force multiplying/dividing the big numbers instead of cancelling first, that's worth a quick "nice, now watch this faster way" rather than marking it fully solid.

Part 1 — Division: rewrite as a fraction, cancel a common factor before you divide
1.
144 ÷ 16 = ?
Answer: 9. 144/16 — both divisible by 16: 9. (Or cancel an 8 first: 18/2 = 9.)
2.
182 ÷ 14 = ?
Answer: 13. 182/14 — both divisible by 14: 13. (Or cancel a 2 first: 91/7 = 13.)
3.
315 ÷ 45 = ?
Answer: 7. 315/45 — both divisible by 45: 7. (Or cancel a 9 first: 35/5 = 7.)
4.
96 ÷ 6 = ?
Answer: 16. Small numbers — just divide directly, 96 ÷ 6 = 16. Hunting for a bigger shared factor here wastes time, not saves it. Knowing when not to bother is half the skill.
5.
1,120 ÷ 80 = ?
Answer: 14. 1120/80 — cancel a 10 first: 112/8 = 14.
6.
221 ÷ 17 = ?
Answer: 13. 17 × 13 = 221. Not an obvious shared factor at a glance — this is a case where testing a couple of multiples of 17 (or just running the division) beats hunting for a "nice" number. Recognizing this isn't a cancel-first case is itself the judgment call.
7.
2,160 ÷ 144 = ?
Answer: 15. 2160/144 — cancel a 16 first: 135/9 = 15.
8.
78 ÷ 6 = ?
Answer: 13. Small numbers again — direct division (78 ÷ 6 = 13) is just as fast as simplifying first.
Part 2 — Multiplying by a fraction: cancel a common factor between the whole number and the denominator first
9.
21 × 47 = ?
Answer: 12. 21 and the denominator 7 share a factor of 7 — cancel first: 21÷7=3, then 3×4=12. Much faster than 21×4=84, then 84÷7.
10.
18 × 56 = ?
Answer: 15. 18 and 6 share a factor of 6 — cancel first: 18÷6=3, then 3×5=15.
11.
35 × 35 = ?
Answer: 21. 35 and 5 share a factor of 5 — cancel first: 35÷5=7, then 7×3=21.
12.
24 × 78 = ?
Answer: 21. 24 and 8 share a factor of 8 — cancel first: 24÷8=3, then 3×7=21.
13.
45 × 29 = ?
Answer: 10. 45 and 9 share a factor of 9 — cancel first: 45÷9=5, then 5×2=10.
14.
28 × 57 = ?
Answer: 20. 28 and 7 share a factor of 7 — cancel first: 28÷7=4, then 4×5=20.
15.
36 × 512 = ?
Answer: 15. 36 and 12 share a factor of 12 — cancel first: 36÷12=3, then 3×5=15.
16.
50 × 710 = ?
Answer: 35. 50 and 10 share a factor of 10 — cancel first: 50÷10=5, then 5×7=35.
