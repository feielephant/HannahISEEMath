On a map, 2 centimeters stand for 15 kilometers. Two towns are 9 centimeters apart on the map. How far apart are the real towns?
Her answer on 11/17: D

2.  Set 11 #14
The thousands cube shown has a volume of 1,000 cubic centimeters. Exactly 20 of these thousands cubes are used to build a new figure. Which expression shows the volume of the new figure, in cubic centimeters?
Her answer on 11/17: B
Answer: C. 20 × 1,000 = 20,000. 20 = 2 × 10 and 1,000 = 10 × 10 × 10, so 20,000 = 2 × 10 × 10 × 10 × 10 (four 10s). B is only 2,000.
Ask her: How many 10s are in 20? How many in 1,000? So how many in 20 × 1,000?
3.  Set 11 #15
A store has 25% off everything. The discount on a backpack is $9. Rosa buys the backpack at the sale price and pays with a $40 bill. How much change does Rosa get?
Her answer on 11/17: D
Answer: C. $9 is 25% (one fourth) of the regular price, so the regular price is 4 × $9 = $36. Sale price: $36 − $9 = $27. Change: $40 − $27 = $13. $31 takes only the discount from $40. $4 uses the regular price. $9 stops at the discount.
Ask her: You first picked C, then D. Which one did you mean? What is the regular price?
4.  Set 11 #17
The width of a rectangle is the length divided by 3. The perimeter of the rectangle is 48 feet. What is the area of the rectangle?
Her answer on 11/17: D
Answer: C. Length = 3 widths. Perimeter = width + 3 widths + width + 3 widths = 8 widths = 48, so the width is 6 and the length is 18. Area = 18 × 6 = 108. 144 treats it as a 12 by 12 square. 36 is 6 × 6.
Ask her: If the width is 6, what is the length? Is it a square?
5.  Set 11 #23
A sequence begins 7, 11, 15, 19, … What is the 25th number in the sequence?
Her answer on 11/17: A
Answer: B. The 1st number is 7. To reach the 25th you add 4 only 24 times (one fewer than 25). 7 + 24 × 4 = 7 + 96 = 103. 107 adds 4 twenty-five times, the off-by-one slip.
Ask her: How many jumps of 4 from the 1st number to the 2nd? To the 25th?

7.  Set 11 #30
Nora spent 313 hours at the park. Of that time, 125 hours were spent eating lunch and riding the bus. How many minutes did Nora spend playing? (1 hour = 60 minutes)
Her answer on 11/17: A
Answer: B. 3 1/3 hours = 180 + 20 = 200 minutes. 2/5 of an hour = 60 ÷ 5 × 2 = 24 minutes, so 1 2/5 hours = 84 minutes. 200 − 84 = 116. 176 forgets the 1 whole hour in 1 2/5. 84 is the lunch-and-bus time. 200 is the whole park time.

A shop had 300 bracelets. On Saturday, 40% of the bracelets were sold. Of the bracelets that were NOT sold, 13 were blue. How many of the unsold bracelets were blue?
Answer: B. 40% of 300 = 120 sold, so 300 − 120 = 180 were not sold. 1/3 of 180 = 60. 40 takes 1/3 of the sold bracelets. 100 takes 1/3 of all 300. 120 stops at the number sold.

A jar has red, blue, and green beads. There are 4 times as many red beads as blue beads. There are 2 times as many blue beads as green beads. There are 66 beads in all. How many red beads are in the jar?
Answer: D. Use parts. Green = 1 part. Blue = 2 times green = 2 parts. Red = 4 times blue = 8 parts. In all: 1 + 2 + 8 = 11 parts = 66, so 1 part = 6. Red = 8 × 6 = 48. 12 is blue and 6 is green (stopping one step early). 33 just halves 66.
