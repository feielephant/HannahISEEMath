1.
Nina builds picture frames. The first rectangular frame she builds has a perimeter of 26 inches. The length of one of the sides is shown. For her next frame, she keeps the shorter side the same length. The longer side is longer than the shorter side by 2 times the length of the shorter side. What is the perimeter of the next frame?
A  15 inchesB  20 inchesC  30 inchesD  40 inches
Answer: D. Two sides of 8 use 16 inches, so the other two sides share 26 − 16 = 10, and each is 5. So 8 is the longer side and 5 is the shorter side. In the next frame the shorter side stays 5. The longer side is 5 PLUS 2 × 5 more: 5 + 10 = 15, which is 3 times the shorter side. Perimeter: 2 × (5 + 15) = 40. C makes the longer side only 2 times the shorter side (10), missing the "longer than ... by" part. A is the new longer side. B is one length plus one width.

Answer: B. Go around the outside and count the square sides: there are 18. 36 ÷ 18 = 2, so each square is 2 by 2 with area 4. Eight squares: 8 × 4 = 32. Mark each side with a tick as you count it. On QR9 #10 she counted 11 sides where there were 10.

8.
Sally chooses a number at random from a hat containing integers from 10 - 20. The number is prime. When this number is subtracted from 20, the result is also prime. The largest number in the hat is divisible by the sum of the chosen number's digits. What is the number that Sally chose?
A  11B  13C  17D  19
Answer: B. Check each choice against every rule, one at a time, and cross it out as soon as one rule fails. All four are prime. 20 − 11 = 9 and 20 − 19 = 1 are not prime, so 11 and 19 are out. Digit sums: 13 → 4 and 17 → 8. 20 ÷ 4 = 5, but 20 ÷ 8 is not whole, so the answer is 13.

9.
Ted picks a two-digit prime number where the first digit is less than the second digit. The difference of its digits is also prime and is a factor of the sum of its digits. Both of these results are less than 6. What is the two-digit prime that Ted chose?
A  11B  13C  23D  31
Answer: B. First digit less than second: 11 (equal) and 31 are out. 13: difference 2 (prime), sum 4, 2 is a factor of 4, both less than 6. 23: difference 1, which is not prime. So 13. Test every rule on every choice that is left.

10.
The price of a game is increased by 50%. The new price is then decreased by 20%. What is the overall percent increase or percent decrease from the original price?
A  percent decrease by 10%B  percent increase by 30%C  percent increase by 70%D  percent increase by 20%
Answer: D. Start with $100. Up 50%: 100 + 50 = 150. Down 20% of 150: 150 − 30 = 120. 120 is 20 more than 100, so it is a 20% increase. B just subtracts the percents (50 − 20). C adds them.

A  18%B  30%C  36%D  45%
Answer: C. The grid is 10 squares across and 5 down, so there are 50 squares. 18 are shaded. 18/50 = 36/100 = 36%. A reads the count as the percent. D counts only 8 columns (18/40). Count the rows and columns of the whole grid first, then the shaded squares, writing each number down.

12.
Ava had □ stickers and then she gave ○ of them to Ben. Now, Ben has △ more stickers than Ava had. Which expression represents the number of stickers Ben had to begin with?
A  □ + △ − ○B  □ + △ + ○C  △ − □ − ○D  □ − ○ − △
Answer: A. Ava HAD □. Ben now has △ more than that: □ + △. Before Ava gave him ○, he had ○ fewer: □ + △ − ○. Try numbers: □ = 10, ○ = 3, △ = 5. Ben now has 15, so he started with 12. Only A gives 12.

14.
In a sequence, each number is 7 more than the number before it. The 9th number in the sequence is 65. What is the 25th number in the sequence?
A  112B  170C  175D  177
Answer: D. From the 9th to the 25th number is 25 − 9 = 16 steps. 16 × 7 = 112, and 65 + 112 = 177. A is 16 × 7, one step early: it still needs adding to 65. B uses 15 steps. C is 25 × 7.

16.
The length of AD is 15. The length of AB is x. The length of CD is y. What is the length of BC?
A  15 − x + yB  x + y − 15C  15 + x − yD  15 − (x + y)
Answer: D. BC is what is left of AD after taking away AB and CD: 15 − x − y. Taking away x and then y is the same as taking away (x + y), so 15 − (x + y). A is the parentheses removed wrong: a minus sign in front of the parentheses changes the sign of EVERYTHING inside, so −(x + y) = −x − y, not −x + y.

17.
If the perimeter of a square is increased from 12 centimeters to 16 centimeters, then by what amount will its area increase?
A  4 centimeters²B  6 centimeters²C  7 centimeters²D  9 centimeters²
Answer: C. Perimeter 12 means a side of 3, area 9. Perimeter 16 means a side of 4, area 16. The area grows by 16 − 9 = 7. A is the change in perimeter, not area.

18.
The length of AC is x. The length of BD is y. The length of AD is 15. What is the length of BC?
A  x − (15 − y)B  15 − x − yC  x − 15 − yD  x + y + 15
Answer: A. AC and BD together cover all of AD plus BC a second time, so x + y = 15 + BC and BC = x + y − 15. A is the same thing: x − (15 − y) = x − 15 + y, because the minus in front of the parentheses flips −y to +y. C removes the parentheses without flipping the sign. Check with A = 0, B = 4, C = 9, D = 15: x = 9, y = 11, BC = 5, and A gives 9 − 4 = 5.

21.
A survey was done to determine which sport was preferred between soccer, football, and basketball. There were 3 times as many votes for soccer as football and 5 times as many votes for football as for basketball. If there is a total of 42 people in the survey, how many people voted for soccer?
A  24B  27C  30D  35
Answer: C. Let basketball be 1 part. Football is 5 parts and soccer is 3 × 5 = 15 parts: 1 + 5 + 15 = 21 parts = 42, so 1 part = 2. Soccer = 15 × 2 = 30.
