2.
A pair of shoes cost $70. The price was decreased by 30%. Then Leo used a $6 coupon on the new price. How much did Leo pay for the shoes?
A  $34B  $43C  $49D  $63.70
Answer: B. 30% of 70 is 0.30 × 70 = 21, so the new price is 70 − 21 = 49. After the coupon, 49 − 6 = 43. D subtracts 0.30 instead of 30% of the price. C stops before the coupon. A takes off $30 instead of 30%.

3.
○ + ○ + △ = 17
○ + △ + △ = 13
What is the value of ○?
A  3B  4C  7D  10
Answer: C. Add the two lines: three ○ and three △ make 17 + 13 = 30, so ○ + △ = 10. The first line is ○ + (○ + △) = 17, so ○ + 10 = 17 and ○ = 7. Check the second line: 7 + 3 + 3 = 13. Another way: ○ = 13 − 2△, put it in the first line twice: 26 − 4△ + △ = 17, so 3△ = 9 and △ = 3. A is △. B is the difference ○ − △. D is ○ + △ (stopping one step early).
