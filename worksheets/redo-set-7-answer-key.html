4 Time-Zone Elapsed Time
Problem 6
Rome is 6 hours ahead of Boston. If a plane leaves Rome at 8:00 A.M. and arrives in Boston 11 hours later, what time is it in Boston when the plane arrives?
AnswerC
Why: Boston is 6 hours behind Rome, so departure time in Boston is 8:00 A.M. minus 6 hours; adding the 11-hour flight gives 1:00 P.M..

6 Composite Shapes: Volume & Area
Problem 10
The figure shown was made by joining two rectangular prisms. Which expression can be used to find the total volume of the figure? (V = lwh)
AnswerA
Why: The tall block has a 2×2 footprint and stands 10 ft tall: 2×2×10=40 cubic ft. The shorter block extends 5 ft further, is 2 ft deep, and stands 4 ft tall: 5×2×4=40 cubic ft. Total = (2×2×10) + (5×2×4) = 80 cubic ft.

Problem 12
The figure below is made up of two overlapping squares. The side length of the larger square is 11 ft, and the area of the shaded region is 85 ft². What is the side length of the smaller square?
Answer6
Why: The larger square's area is 11×11=121 ft². The unshaded (smaller square) area is 121−85=36 ft², so the smaller square's side is √36=6 ft.

Problem 14
The perimeter of the largest polygon is 24 centimeters. The perimeter of the square is 20 centimeters. Use the figure to answer the question. What is the perimeter of the triangle?
Answer14
Why: The square's perimeter is 20 cm, so each side is 5 cm. The shared side between the square and triangle is counted once in each shape's own perimeter but isn't part of the combined figure's outer edge: 24 = 20 + (triangle's perimeter) − 2×5, giving a triangle perimeter of 14 cm.

2 Number-Machine Tables
Problem 16
A number machine uses a rule to change numbers. The table shows what happened when four different numbers went into the same number machine. Which of these could be four more results from the same number machine?
AnswerB
Why: The machine's rule is “multiply by 4, then subtract 2” (4×3−2=10, 4×5−2=18, 4×6−2=22, 4×9−2=34). Applying that same rule to 4,6,7,8 gives 14,22,26,30, which is choice B.

3 Divisibility Rules
Problem 21
Which number is divisible by 6?
AnswerC
Why: A number divisible by 6 must be divisible by both 2 and 3. 612 is even and its digits sum to 9 (a multiple of 3), so 612÷6=102 exactly. 514, 728, and 842 are all even but their digit sums (10, 17, 14) aren't multiples of 3.
Problem 22
Which number is divisible by 9?
AnswerD
Why: A number is divisible by 9 if its digits sum to a multiple of 9. 4+0+1+4=9, so 4,014÷9=446 exactly. The digit sums for 5,023 (10), 8,117 (17), and 3,140 (8) are not multiples of 9.
Problem 23
Which number is divisible by 4?
AnswerD
Why: A number is divisible by 4 if its last two digits form a number divisible by 4. 2,316 ends in 16, and 16÷4=4 exactly, so 2,316÷4=579. The last two digits of 1,234 (34), 3,127 (27), and 4,501 (01) aren't divisible by 4.
