Which expression has the same value as 40 − (15 + 9 − 4)?
A  40 − 15 − 9 + 4B  40 − 15 + 9 − 4C  40 − 15 − 9 − 4D  40 + 15 − 9 + 4
Answer: A. The minus in front takes away the whole group: 15 + 9 − 4 = 20, and 40 − 20 = 20. Taking away the group flips each sign inside: −15, −9, +4. A = 20. C forgets to flip the −4 (it gives 12).
