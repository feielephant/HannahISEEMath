1.  QR12 #2
A coupon gives 20% off the regular price of a jacket. The coupon saves Mia $4. What is the regular price of the jacket?
Her answer on 11/27: A (39 seconds)
Answer: C. The question asks for: regular price. 20% is $4. 100% is 5 times 20%, so the regular price is 5 × $4 = $20. (Whole = part ÷ percent: 4 ÷ 0.2 = 20.)
Wrong choices: A: Known trap (her pick on the real one): doubled the $4 instead of finding 100% · B: Tripled the $4 · D: Added the $4 to the regular price
Ask her: If 20% is $4, what is 40%? 60%? Keep going: how many 20%s make 100%?
2.  QR12 #4
The design, when completed, has one line of symmetry.
Which tile, without rotating, correctly completes the design?
Her answer on 11/27: D (24 seconds)
Answer: A. The question asks for: the tile that makes it symmetric. The line of symmetry is horizontal (through the middle tile and its black square on the left side). The top row mirrors the bottom row. The missing top-right tile is the bottom-right tile flipped top to bottom: its diagonal goes the other way and its black square moves from the top-right corner to the bottom-right corner. Check with the left column: top-left and bottom-left are mirror images too.
Wrong choices: D: Copied the bottom-right tile without flipping it · B: Flipped it the wrong way (left to right): right diagonal, square in the opposite corner (like her pick on the real one) · C: Kept the diagonal and moved the square: not a mirror image
Ask her: Where is the line of symmetry? Put your finger on the bottom-right tile and flip it over that line: which way does the diagonal go now? Which corner is the square in?
3.  QR12 #14
Dana wants to cover a patio with tiles. Her patio is 120 inches long and 60 inches wide. Each tile is 6 inches long and 5 inches wide. If she knows how to find the area of a rectangle, how can Dana determine how many tiles she will need without laying them all on the patio? (A = l × w)
Her answer on 11/27: D (92 seconds)
Answer: A. The question asks for: number of tiles (expression). Tiles along the length: 120 ÷ 6 = 20. Tiles along the width: 60 ÷ 5 = 12. Total tiles = (120 ÷ 6) × (60 ÷ 5) = 240.
Wrong choices: B: Subtracted squares of the tile sides (her pick type on the real one) · C: Subtracted instead of divided · D: Divided by the squares of the tile sides
Ask her: How many tiles fit along the 120-inch side if each tile is 6 inches long? Why would you use 6 × 6?
4.  QR12 #17
A pitcher holds 3.4 liters of juice. Maria pours three cups of juice, totaling 34 liter. How much juice is left in the pitcher?
Her answer on 11/27: D (129 seconds)
Answer: C. The question asks for: how much is left. 3/4 liter = 0.75 liter. 3.4 − 0.75 = 2.65 liters, which is more than 2.5 and less than 3.
Wrong choices: A: Read the 3/4 as what is left (her pick on the real one) · B: Took away 1 whole liter instead of 3/4 · D: Took away 0.34 or 0.4
Ask her: Write 3/4 as a decimal first. Is 3/4 the same as 0.34? Then subtract from 3.4.
5.  QR12 #21
At a school fair, raffle tickets are numbered from 120 to 160. Every ticket whose number is divisible by 6 wins a prize. How many tickets win a prize?
Her answer on 11/27: B (93 seconds)
Answer: C. The question asks for: how many winning tickets. The tickets start at 120, and 120 ÷ 6 = 20, so 120 wins. Count by 6s: 120, 126, 132, 138, 144, 150, 156 (162 is past 160). That is 7 tickets.
Wrong choices: A: Missed both ends · B: Left out ticket 120 (it is divisible by 6 and is on the list) · D: Estimated 40 ÷ 6 and rounded up, or counted 162
Ask her: Write the first winning ticket. Is 120 divisible by 6? Is 120 on the list (from 120 to 160)?
6.  QR12 #27
Ana has a bag of marbles in different colors: blue, red, green, and yellow. The probability of picking a blue marble is 2 out of 7. What combination of marbles reflects this probability?
Her answer on 11/27: C (44 seconds)
Answer: B. The question asks for: blue out of ALL the marbles. 2 out of 7 means 2 blue for every 7 marbles in all, so 2 blue and 5 others. 4 blue and 10 others is 4 out of 14 = 2/7.
Wrong choices: A: Known trap (39.5% picked this on the real one): used 7 as the OTHER marbles, not the total · C: Doubled the 2 and the 7 but used 14 as the others · D: 6 out of 20 = 3/10, not 2/7
Ask her: "2 out of 7": out of 7 what? How many marbles in all? How many are NOT blue?
