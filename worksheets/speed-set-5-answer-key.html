The figure is composed of a square and a triangle, sharing one side. The square has a side length of 7 cm. The triangle's other two sides are 9 cm and 12 cm. What is the perimeter of the whole figure?
A  28 cmB  35 cmC  42 cmD  49 cm
Answer: C. The square contributes 3 outer sides (the 4th side is shared/internal): 3 × 7 = 21 cm. The triangle contributes its 2 non-shared sides: 9 + 12 = 21 cm. Total: 21 + 21 = 42 cm.
