The figure is composed of a square and a triangle, sharing one side. The combined outer perimeter of the whole figure is 42 cm. The square's own perimeter is 24 cm. What is the triangle's own perimeter?
A  18 cmB  24 cmC  30 cmD  36 cm
Answer: C. The square's side length is 24 ÷ 4 = 6 cm. The square contributes 3 of its sides to the outer perimeter (the 4th is shared/internal): 3 × 6 = 18 cm. That leaves 42 − 18 = 24 cm for the triangle's 2 non-shared sides. The triangle's OWN full perimeter also includes the shared side: 24 + 6 = 30 cm.

A  53B  69C  72D  75
Answer: C. Look at the differences between terms: 7−3=4, 14−7=7, 24−14=10, 37−24=13. Each difference is 3 more than the last (4,7,10,13,16,19,...). 6th term: 37+16=53. 7th term: 53+19=72. 🚚
