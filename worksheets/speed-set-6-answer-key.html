What is the next number in the sequence? 2, 3, 5, 8, 12, 17, 23, ...
A  24B  30C  26D  32
Answer: B. The differences between terms grow by 1 each time: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. The next difference is 7, so the next term is 23 + 7 = 30.

A  5B  32C  10D  34
Answer: C. The ratio of common to rare is 8:2, which simplifies to 4:1. 40 common cards ÷ 4 = 10 groups, so Chloe has 10 rare cards (not 40 − 8 = 32, which mixes up what's being asked).

The figure is composed of a regular pentagon and a square, sharing one side. All labeled sides measure 3 inches. What is the perimeter of the whole figure?
A  18 inchesB  24 inchesC  21 inchesD  27 inches
Answer: C. The pentagon has 5 sides of 3 in, but 1 side is shared/internal (where it meets the square), so it contributes 4 sides to the outer perimeter: 4 × 3 = 12 in. The square has 4 sides of 3 in, also sharing 1 side with the pentagon, so it contributes 3 sides: 3 × 3 = 9 in. Total outer perimeter: 12 + 9 = 21 in.
