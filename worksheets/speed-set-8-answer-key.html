A  35B  36C  37D  38
Answer: C. The differences between terms are 3, 5, 7, 9 -- each difference is 2 more than the last (not constant). The next difference is 11, so the 6th term is 26 + 11 = 37. Checking only the first difference (3) and assuming it stays constant is the trap here.

Mia cut three lengths of ribbon. Ribbon 1 measured 2 yards, 2 feet, 4 inches. Ribbon 2 measured 4 yards, 1 inch. Ribbon 3 measured 1 yard, 7 feet, 2 inches. Which statement is true? (1 yard = 3 feet; 1 foot = 12 inches)
A  Ribbon 1 is longer than Ribbon 3.B  Ribbon 3 is longer than Ribbon 2.C  Ribbon 2 is longer than Ribbon 1.D  Ribbon 1 and Ribbon 3 are the same length.
Answer: C. Convert each to inches. Ribbon 1: 2 yd = 72 in, + 2 ft (24 in) + 4 in = 100 in. Ribbon 2: 4 yd = 144 in, + 1 in = 145 in. Ribbon 3: 7 ft = 84 in = 2 yd 1 ft, so 1 yd + 7 ft = 3 yd 1 ft; total = 108 in + 12 in + 2 in = 122 in. So Ribbon 1 = 100 in, Ribbon 3 = 122 in, Ribbon 2 = 145 in. Ribbon 2 (145) is longer than Ribbon 1 (100) -- true. The other three statements are all false once everything is converted to the same unit.

Answer: B. Let the original fee be 100. After a 20% increase: 100 × 1.2 = 120. After a 20% decrease: 120 × 0.8 = 96. That's a net change of 100 → 96, a 4% decrease. The two 20% changes do NOT cancel out, because the second 20% is taken from a larger (then smaller) base.

A  24B  16C  27D  20
Answer: A. z must be a multiple of 36 (since 4 and 9 share no common factor, the smallest number divisible by both is 4×9=36). So 2z is a multiple of 72. 24 divides 72 evenly (72 ÷ 24 = 3), so 24 always divides 2z. 16, 27, and 20 do not divide 72 evenly.
