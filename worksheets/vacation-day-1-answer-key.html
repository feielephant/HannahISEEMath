A store is having a sale that ends on Sunday. Every jacket is 30% off its regular price, and every hat is 20% off. For one jacket, the discount is $18. What is the sale price of this jacket?
A  $12.60B  $42C  $60D  $78
Answer: B. The question asks for: sale price of the jacket. $18 is 30% of the regular price. 10% is $18 ÷ 3 = $6, so 100% is $60. The sale price is $60 − $18 = $42. The hat discount and "Sunday" are extra information.

3.
A square has a perimeter of 36 centimeters. A rectangle has the same area as the square. The length of the rectangle is 27 centimeters. What is the perimeter of the rectangle?
A  3 centimetersB  30 centimetersC  60 centimetersD  81 centimeters
Answer: C. The question asks for: perimeter of the rectangle. Square side = 36 ÷ 4 = 9, so its area is 9 × 9 = 81. The rectangle has area 81 and length 27, so its width is 81 ÷ 27 = 3. Perimeter = 27 + 3 + 27 + 3 = 60.
Wrong choices: A: Stopped early: this is the width of the rectangle · B: Stopped early: length + width, only half the perimeter · D: Stopped early: this is the area (and the wrong unit)
