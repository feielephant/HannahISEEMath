1.  Day 1 #2
Cube A has edges that are 2 centimeters long. Cube B is 3 times as wide as Cube A. How many cubic centimeters greater is the volume of Cube B than the volume of Cube A?
Her answer on 11/24: A (58 seconds)
Answer: C. The question asks for: how much greater (a difference). Cube B has edges 2 × 3 = 6 cm. Volume of B = 6 × 6 × 6 = 216. Volume of A = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8. 216 − 8 = 208. (27 Cube A's fit inside B, but the question asks for the difference.)
Wrong choices: A: Known trap: thought 3 times as wide means 3 times the volume (24 − 8) · B: Answered the real test's question: how many Cube A's fit · D: Stopped early: this is the volume of Cube B
Ask her: Cube B is 3 times as WIDE. How long is each edge of Cube B? Now find the volume of each cube.
2.  Day 1 #5
Leo had □ stickers. He gave ○ of them to Ava. Now Ava has △ times as many stickers as Leo has left. Which expression represents the number of stickers Ava had to begin with?
Her answer on 11/24: B (26 seconds)
Answer: D. The question asks for: Ava, at the beginning. Timeline. After the gift, Leo has □ − ○ left, and Ava has △ times that: △ × (□ − ○). Before the gift, Ava had ○ fewer: △ × (□ − ○) − ○. Teaching tip (parent 11/21: she did not get the idea on MA6 #15): draw "before | gift | now" and fill in Leo first, then Ava, then go back one step.
Wrong choices: A: Answered the real test's version ("as he HAD"), not "has left" · B: Stopped early: this is how many Ava has NOW · C: Went the wrong way in time: added the gift instead of taking it back
Ask her: Draw before | gift | now. How many does Leo have left? How many does Ava have NOW? Then how many did she have BEFORE the gift?
3.  Day 1 #6
How many whole numbers between 50 and 150 are divisible by both 6 and 9?
Her answer on 11/24: C (178 seconds)
Answer: B. The question asks for: how many numbers. Step 1, the smallest number divisible by both 6 and 9 (the LCM): 18. Step 2, count the multiples of 18 between 50 and 150: 54, 72, 90, 108, 126, 144 = 6.
Wrong choices: A: Known trap: used 6 × 9 = 54 instead of the LCM 18 · C: Checked only 9 · D: Stopped early: this is the LCM, not how many
Ask her: Which numbers are divisible by 6 AND by 9? What is the smallest one? Now count its multiples between 50 and 150.

Students wrote a new number using the digits 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, and 9 and these rules. What digit is in the tens place of the new number?
RULES FOR THE NEW NUMBER
1. The number must have 6 digits.
2. The number must be odd.
3. The number must be the largest number possible.
4. Each digit can be used only once.
Answer: A. The question asks for: the tens digit. Largest means the big digits go first. Odd means the last digit is 3, 5, 7 or 9; to keep the front as big as possible, put the SMALLEST odd digit (3) last. The number is 987,523. Tens place: 2. Write the number with the place names under it before answering.
Wrong choices: B: Forgot the number must be odd (987,532) · C: Read the hundreds place (her QR11 #35 habit) · D: Read the thousands place
