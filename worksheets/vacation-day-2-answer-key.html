A store sells gift boxes shaped like cubes. The table shows the edge length of each size. How many Small boxes together have the same volume as one Large box?
GIFT BOXES
Box sizeEdge length
Small3 inches
Medium6 inches
Large9 inches
A  3B  9C  27D  729
Answer: C. The question asks for: how many Small boxes. The Large edge is 3 times the Small edge (9 ÷ 3). Volume grows in all three directions: 3 × 3 × 3 = 27. Check: Large = 9 × 9 × 9 = 729, Small = 3 × 3 × 3 = 27, and 729 ÷ 27 = 27. The Medium row is extra information.

A sign is made of a square and a triangle that share one side, as shown. The two slanted sides of the triangle are the same length. The perimeter of the whole sign is 30 inches. The perimeter of the square alone is 24 inches. What is the perimeter of the triangle alone?
A  12 inchesB  18 inchesC  24 inchesD  30 inches
Answer: B. The question asks for: perimeter of the triangle alone. Square side = 24 ÷ 4 = 6. The outside of the sign has 3 square sides (18) and the 2 slanted sides, so the slanted sides add to 30 − 18 = 12. The triangle alone also has the shared side: 12 + 6 = 18.
