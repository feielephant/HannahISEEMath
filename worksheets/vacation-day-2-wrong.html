1.  Day 2 #3
Mr. Lee had □ books on his shelf. Ms. Diaz gave him ○ books from her shelf. Now Ms. Diaz has △ fewer books than Mr. Lee has. Which expression represents the number of books Ms. Diaz had at first?
Her answer on 11/25: B (50 seconds)
Answer: A. The question asks for: Ms. Diaz, at first. Timeline (before | gift | now). Now Mr. Lee has □ + ○. Ms. Diaz has △ fewer: □ + ○ − △. Before she gave away ○ books she had ○ more: □ + ○ − △ + ○.
Wrong choices: B: Stopped early: this is how many Ms. Diaz has NOW · C: Forgot Mr. Lee got ○ more books before comparing · D: Went the wrong way in time: took the gift away again instead of giving it back
Ask her: Draw before | gift | now. How many does Mr. Lee have now? Ms. Diaz now? She GAVE books away, so at first did she have more or fewer?
2.  Day 2 #4
A sign is made of a square and a triangle that share one side, as shown. The two slanted sides of the triangle are the same length. The perimeter of the whole sign is 30 inches. The perimeter of the square alone is 24 inches. What is the perimeter of the triangle alone?
Her answer on 11/25: A (70 seconds)
Answer: B. The question asks for: perimeter of the triangle alone. Square side = 24 ÷ 4 = 6. The outside of the sign has 3 square sides (18) and the 2 slanted sides, so the slanted sides add to 30 − 18 = 12. The triangle alone also has the shared side: 12 + 6 = 18.
Wrong choices: A: Stopped early: this is only the two slanted sides · C: Gave the square's perimeter · D: Gave the whole sign's perimeter
Ask her: You found 12. Which sides are those? Does the triangle alone have any other side?
3.  Day 2 #6
Lena has a notebook with pages numbered 1 to 200. She puts a star on every 4th page (page 4, 8, 12, and so on) and a heart on every 10th page (page 10, 20, 30, and so on). She also puts a dot on every page that is a multiple of 3. How many pages have both a star and a heart?
Her answer on 11/25: C (66 seconds)
Answer: B. The question asks for: how many pages. A page with both is a multiple of 4 AND of 10. The smallest such page is 20 (the LCM), so the pages are 20, 40, ..., 200. 200 ÷ 20 = 10 pages. The dots are extra information.
Wrong choices: A: Known trap (MA6 #27): used 4 × 10 = 40 instead of the LCM · C: Stopped early: this is the LCM (every 20th page), not how many · D: Counted the stars only (Day 1: counted one number only)
Ask her: You found 20. What does 20 stand for? The question asks HOW MANY pages. How many multiples of 20 are there up to 200?
